HOCO (Homecoming) FAQ

Everything About HOCO Shopping

Located at 4701 N. University St, Peoria, IL 61614 · Open Mon & Fri 12–6pm, Tue & Thu 12–7pm, Sat 10am–5pm (Closed Wed & Sun).

What is the price range for HOCO dresses?

Our homecoming dresses range from $90 to $580 across short and long styles.

Are HOCO dresses in stock or special order?

Our dresses are in stock and available for immediate same-day purchase. Special order options are also available on select styles and sizes.

Do you carry accessories to complete my look?

Yes. We carry statement earrings in store to pair with your dress (we don't carry shoes or clutches).

Do I need an appointment?

No appointment needed. Cloud Nine Prom is walk-in only and first come, first serve. Fill out a short check-in form when you arrive and a stylist will match you with a fitting room.
