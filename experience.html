Everything You Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about booking, appointment etiquette, or guest guidelines? Here are helpful answers from our styling team.

We recommend shopping 9 to 12 months before your wedding date. Made-to-order designer gowns typically take 4 to 6 months to arrive from the atelier, leaving 2 to 3 months for custom alterations. If your wedding is closer, we also offer rush orders and off-the-rack selections.
Our private suites comfortably accommodate the bride plus 4 to 6 guests. We recommend bringing your closest decision-makers (mom, maid of honor, trusted family) whose opinions you value most to keep your experience intimate, joyful, and focused on you.
We recommend wearing nude seamless undergarments or shapewear, and a strapless bra if preferred (many gowns feature built-in boning and cups). If you have specific wedding heels or heirloom jewelry in mind, feel free to bring them along!
Our curated bridal gowns range from $1,200 to $3,800, with the sweet spot of our collection falling between $1,500 and $2,400. We also host seasonal sample sales and trunk show events.
Yes! When scheduling your appointment online or by phone, you can choose between our Complimentary Signature Suite, Robes & Rosé VIP ($100), or On Cloud Nine Ultra-VIP ($250). VIP upgrades include chilled bubbly, artisanal sweet treats, robes, and extended fitting time.
