About Cloud Nine · Est. 2011

15 Years of Saying Yes

Cloud Nine Bridal Boutique in Peoria IL is the ultimate destination for brides who cherish their individual and unique style! From boho chic to classic and timeless, we have handpicked unique gowns from the world’s finest designers. You will discover a vast variety of sizes and silhouettes from award winning looks by Essense of Australia, Stella York, Maggie Sottero, Sottero & Midgley, Rebecca Ingram, and our very own Rose + Grace Collection. With dresses ranging from $1200 to $4000, you are sure to find the perfect dress within your budget.

Beyond bridal, we are also dedicated to helping Mother's of the Bride and Groom feel confident for the special day as well as helping the groom and groomsmen look sharp in their tuxes or suits!

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have plus sizes? +

Yes! At Cloud Nine Bridal Boutique, we carry in-store sample sizes 6–28, and we proudly carry inclusive lines including Essense of Australia EveryBody EveryBride and Stella York EveryBody EveryBride.

Do I need an appointment? +

Yes! To ensure you receive one-on-one attention from an expert stylist, appointments are required at our Bridal Boutique. You can easily schedule online or call us at (309) 693-3830. Standard bridal appointments are complimentary, and we also offer upgraded experiences including Robes & Rosé ($100) and On Cloud Nine ($250).

How long are appointments? +

Standard Bridal and Robes & Rosé appointments are 90 minutes long, giving you plenty of time to work with your stylist and try on gowns. Our Ultimate "On Cloud Nine" VIP appointment is an extended 2-hour experience (available Monday–Friday with 48 hours advance notice), and Mothers appointments are 1 hour.

How many guests can I bring? +

For the best experience, we ask that you leave children at home and bring no more than 6 guests. We recommend bringing 3–4 of your closest loved ones so the focus stays entirely on how you feel in each gown.

How much are the gowns on average? +

Cloud Nine wedding gowns start at $1,200 and go up to $4,000, with most brides finding their perfect dress right around the $2,000 mark.

What is the average shipping time? +

Made-to-order wedding gowns typically take 3 to 6 months to arrive once ordered. If your wedding is on a tighter timeline, don't worry—we also have a selection of gorgeous gowns available to purchase directly off the rack!

Do you do alterations? +

We do not offer in-store alterations. However, we provide every bride with a curated list of trusted, experienced bridal seamstresses throughout Central Illinois.

Can I return my dress? What payment methods do you accept? +

Because bridal gowns and formalwear are special-ordered specifically for you, all sales are final and we do not accept returns. We accept cash, credit cards, Apple Pay, and checks (please note that if paying by check, we must wait until the check clears before your gown can be picked up).

When should I book my tux appointment? +

We recommend booking your tuxedo and suit selection appointment 3 to 5 months before the wedding, leaving the last 2 months for wedding party measurements. We also offer Tux-to-Door convenience so out-of-state groomsmen can have their suits delivered right to their doorstep!

How early should mothers shop? +

We recommend Mother of the Bride, Mother of the Groom, and Grandmothers begin shopping at least 6 to 8 months before the wedding date to allow time for browsing, ordering, and any needed alterations.
