Who We Are

18 Years of Saying Yes

Cloud Nine Bridal Boutique has been Peoria's most-loved bridal destination since 2007. We specialize in wedding gowns from the world's finest designers — Essense of Australia, Maggie Sottero, Sottero & Midgley, Rebecca Ingram, Stella York, and our exclusive Rose + Grace Collection.

We offer gowns ranging from $1,200 to $4,000, with most brides finding their perfect dress around $2,000. Every purchase is final sale, and we don't offer in-house alterations — but we provide a curated list of trusted seamstresses throughout Central Illinois.

Beyond bridal, we serve the entire wedding party — mothers, groomsmen, prom, and homecoming. We're a true one-stop shop for every formal occasion.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have plus sizes? +

Yes! At Cloud Nine Bridal Boutique, we carry in-store sample sizes up to size 26. Our designers offer special-order gowns up to size 38, and we carry inclusive lines including Essense of Australia EveryBody EveryBride and Stella York EveryBody EveryBride.

Do I need an appointment? +

Yes — Cloud Nine Bridal operates by appointment to ensure each bride receives personalized attention and an exclusive experience. Standard appointments are free, with elevated experiences like "Robes & Rosé" ($100) and "On Cloud Nine" ($250) available for added celebration.

How long are appointments? +

Standard bridal appointments are 90 minutes long, providing ample time to explore our collection. Your appointment includes a one-on-one bridal stylist, curated gown pulls based on your vision, styling advice, and celebration with your guests.

How many guests can I bring? +

We recommend 3–4 of your closest loved ones for the best experience. Our boutique comfortably accommodates up to 6 guests per bride. For the most relaxing experience, we kindly ask that you leave children at home.

How much are the gowns on average? +

Our bridal gowns range from $1,200 to $4,000, with most brides finding their dream gown around $2,000. We offer a diverse selection across price points without compromising on quality or craftsmanship.

What is the average ordering timeline? +

Gowns typically take 4–6 months to arrive once ordered, though timelines vary by designer. We recommend shopping 9–12 months in advance to allow comfortable time for shipping and alterations.

Do you do alterations? +

Cloud Nine does not perform in-house alterations. We provide our brides with a curated list of trusted, highly experienced Central Illinois seamstresses who specialize in bridal alterations.

Can I return my dress? +

All bridal gowns are special orders made specifically for each bride, so all sales are final. Our stylists take great care during your appointment to ensure you feel 100% confident before ordering.

When should I book my tux appointment? +

We recommend scheduling your group tuxedo appointment 3–5 months before the wedding, with final orders placed approximately 2 months out. Jim's Formal Wear can also ship directly to out-of-town groomsmen's homes!

How early should mothers shop? +

We recommend starting at least 6–8 months before the wedding date to allow time for browsing, ordering, and alterations.
