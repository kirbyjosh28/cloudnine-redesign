Stress-Free Groomsmen Coordination

How Tux-to-Door Works

Have groomsmen scattered across the country? No problem. Through our partnership with Jim's Formal Wear, out-of-town party members get outfitted with zero stress.

01

Groom Builds Look

Visit Cloud Nine in Peoria for your styling fitting, or build your party code online with our team.

02

Nationwide Sizing

Out-of-town groomsmen submit their measurements online or at any local tailor in their hometown.

03

Direct Home Delivery

Each suit ships directly to the groomsman's doorstep 7–10 days before the wedding for a home try-on.

04

Free Easy Return

After saying "Cheers", drop off at Cloud Nine in Peoria or drop in any USPS mailbox with the prepaid label.
