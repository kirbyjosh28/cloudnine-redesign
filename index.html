About Cloud Nine

A Boutique Built
For Your Moment

For over 18 years, Cloud Nine has been the boutique Central Illinois brides drive past every other shop to reach. Our stylists don't just pull dresses — they listen first. To your story, your nerves, your vision. Then they put you in something you hadn't imagined, and watch you catch your breath.

We carry exclusive collections from Maggie Sottero, Essense of Australia, and Rebecca Ingram. Every dress sold here is one of a kind in the area — we carry one style per school for prom, and we protect every bride's moment as fiercely as we celebrate it.

Your Private Fitting Journey

From your first step into our Peoria boutique to the moment you say "YES," here is what your private VIP styling session looks like.

01
Step 01 · Welcome

Private Lounge & Consultation

Settle into your dedicated fitting suite with your bridal party. Meet your stylist, discuss your silhouette vision, wedding vibe, and Lookbook favorites.

02
Step 02 · Selection

Curated Gown Exploration

Explore 600+ handpicked gowns from Maggie Sottero, Essense of Australia, and Stella York with 1-on-1 expert guidance tailored to your body and style.

03
Step 03 · The Fit

360° Pedestal & Veil Moment

Step onto the staging pedestal under natural showroom lighting. Experience custom veil pairings, train styling, and see every intricate lace and bead detail in motion.

When you find the one that speaks to your heart, we celebrate with you. Pop confetti, snap your commemorative photo with the sign, and toast to your dream dress.
