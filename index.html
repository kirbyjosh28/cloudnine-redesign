About Cloud Nine

A Boutique Built
For Your Moment

For over 12 years, Cloud Nine has been the boutique Central Illinois brides drive past every other shop to reach. Our stylists don't just pull dresses — they listen first. To your story, your nerves, your vision. Then they guide you with warmth and excitement into gowns that make your heart skip a beat.

We proudly carry world-class collections from Essense of Australia, Maggie Sottero, Rebecca Ingram, Sottero & Midgley, Stella York, and our exclusive in-house line Rose + Grace ($1,200–$4,000, sizes 0–38). And just steps down at 4701 N. University St, our dedicated Prom Boutique offers 800+ gowns (walk-ins only!).

The In-Store Experience

Your Bridal Styling Journey

From relaxed conversation with your dedicated 1-on-1 stylist to your celebratory "YES!" moment, here is how we make finding your gown effortless and unforgettable.

01
Step 01 · Settle In

Meet Your Stylist & Settle In

Settle into our warm, welcoming boutique with up to 6 of your favorite people (3–4 recommended). Over relaxed conversation, your stylist listens to your wedding vision, venue vibe, budget, and saved favorites before pulling a single gown.

02
Step 02 · The Pulls

Explore Handpicked Silhouettes

No searching through overwhelming racks alone. Your stylist curates gowns across our 6 designer collections tailored to your body and style, offering honest guidance in a supportive, zero-pressure atmosphere.

03
Step 03 · The Reveal

Step Onto the 360° Pedestal

See your gown come alive under natural balanced lighting. We pair custom cathedral veils, headpieces, and belts, adjusting every angle and train so you can feel how the dress moves down the aisle.

04
Step 04 · Celebration

Say YES & Celebrate

When that feeling hits and you know it's the one, we make it unforgettable. Snap your commemorative photo with our iconic sign, toast your milestone, and celebrate your moment with your favorite people.
