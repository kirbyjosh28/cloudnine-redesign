Frequently Asked Questions

Everything About Cloud Nine Prom

Featuring over 1,000 gowns from Amarra, Ashley Lauren, Faviana, Jessica Angel, Johnathan Kayne, Jovani, Rachel Allan, Rose + Grace, Sherri Hill, and Sydney's Prom.

Do I need an appointment to shop?

No appointment needed. Cloud Nine Prom is walk-in only and first come, first serve. When you arrive, you'll complete a quick check-in form so we can match you with a fitting room and a personal stylist.

Where is Cloud Nine Prom located & what are your hours?

Located just steps down from our bridal store at 4701 N. University St, Peoria, IL 61614.
Hours: Mon & Fri 12–6 PM · Tue & Thu 12–7 PM · Sat 10 AM–5 PM · Wed & Sun Closed.

What is the price range for prom & HOCO dresses?

Our prom dresses range from $150 to $1,000, and our homecoming (HOCO) dresses range from $90 to $580.

When should I start shopping for prom?

We recommend starting 2–4 months before prom for the widest selection and time for alterations. Gowns are in stock for immediate same-day purchase, with special orders available on select styles.

Do you offer plus-size prom dresses & accessories?

Yes. We carry sizes 000–36 and 12W–24W (including Sydney's Prom), plus statement earrings in store (we don't carry shoes or clutches).

Do you carry pageant & special occasion dresses?

Yes. We carry gowns for local, state, and national pageants (with special ordering available), plus formalwear for galas, sweet 16s, military balls, and wedding guests.

Do you offer alterations or returns?

We don't offer in-house alterations, but we're happy to refer you to local seamstresses. All sales are final, and our stylists make sure you love the fit before you buy.

Can I bring guests with me?

Yes. Bring your family and friends along. For the most comfortable experience in our fitting area, we recommend bringing a small group of your closest supporters.
