Frequently Asked Questions

Everything About Cloud Nine Prom

Featuring over 1,000 gowns from Amarra, Ashley Lauren, Faviana, Jessica Angel, Johnathan Kayne, Jovani, Rachel Allan, Rose + Grace, Sherri Hill, and Sydney's Prom.

Do I need an appointment to shop?

No appointment needed! Cloud Nine Prom is walk-in only and first come, first serve. When you arrive, you'll fill out a short information form, which helps us match you with a fitting room. From there, one of our stylists will personally assist you with finding the perfect dress.

Where is Cloud Nine Prom located & what are your hours?

Just steps down from our bridal store, you will find our prom location at 4701 N. University St, Peoria, IL 61614.
Hours: Mon & Fri 12:00 PM–6:00 PM | Tue & Thu 12:00 PM–7:00 PM | Sat 10:00 AM–5:00 PM | Wed & Sun CLOSED.

What is the price range for prom & HOCO dresses?

Our prom dresses range from $150 to $1,000, and our homecoming (HOCO) dresses range from $90 to $580, offering options for a variety of budgets and styles.

When should I start shopping for prom?

We recommend starting 2–4 months before prom to get the best selection and allow time for alterations if needed. Our dresses are in stock for immediate same-day purchase, with special order options also available for select styles and sizes!

Do you offer plus-size prom dresses & accessories?

Yes! We are proud to carry an inclusive range of sizes (000–36 and 12W–24W, including Sydney's Prom) so every shopper can feel confident and celebrated. We also carry a curated selection of earrings chosen to pair beautifully with our dresses (we do not carry shoes or clutches).

Do you carry pageant & special occasion dresses?

Yes! We offer a selection of gowns suitable for local, state, and national pageants (with special ordering available based on designer availability and timeline), plus formal gowns for galas, sweet 16s, military balls, wedding guests, and other special occasions.

Do you offer alterations or returns?

We do not offer in-house alterations, however we work closely with expert local seamstresses and will gladly refer you to trusted professionals. All sales are final, and our stylists are dedicated to helping you feel 100% confident in your choice before purchasing.

Can I bring guests with me?

Yes! We love being part of the excitement with your family and friends. For the best experience in our fitting area, we recommend bringing a small group of your closest supporters.
